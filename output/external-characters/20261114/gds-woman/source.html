Description

A huge asset pack of women men characters and hundreds of character map pieces for creating an almost infinite amount of women characters. The pack includes more than twelve premade and fully animated women cartoon style characters including various professions and styles. The animations are included as PNG keyframes and also as fully rigged Spriter puppet. The keyframes are around 650 pixels square although you can export this character using the spriter file up to 2000 pixels before reducing resolution.
