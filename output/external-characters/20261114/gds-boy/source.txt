Description
A massive pack of boy game character sprites including ten fully animated characters and a massive collection of over two hundred and eighty character map pieces for creating an almost unending amount of characters and character variations. The pack contains keyframes of over ten animations (see video) for each of the ten premade characters. Each keyframe measures around 847 pixels by 665 pixels however you can use the included Spriter file to export even larger keyframes up to 2000 pixels before affecting the resolution. You can also use the included png parts or Spriter to make new characters.
