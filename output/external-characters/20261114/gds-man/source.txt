Description
A huge asset pack of various men characters and hundreds of character map pieces for creating an almost infinite amount of men characters. The pack includes 12 premade fully animated men including various professions and generic styled men. The animations are included as PNG keyframes and a fully rigged Spriter puppet. The keyframes are around 650 pixels square although you can export this character using the spriter file up to 2000 pixels before reducing resolution.
