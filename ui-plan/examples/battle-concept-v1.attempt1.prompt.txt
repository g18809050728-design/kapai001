Rich gouache painting with confident dark ink contours, expressive stylized animation-ready character silhouettes, physical paper and book textures, strong foreground/midground/background depth. Deep chalkboard teal, warm parchment, aged brass and restrained burnt-orange accents. Late afternoon golden window light against cool classroom shadows. Finished indie PC game art direction, atmospheric but readable, with weight and personality. Avoid generic fantasy gold filigree. Modern Chinese university setting, not medieval, not photorealistic, not watercolor washed-out beige everywhere.
