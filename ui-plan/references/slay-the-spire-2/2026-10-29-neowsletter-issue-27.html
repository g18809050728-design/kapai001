The Neowsletter - October 2026

October 29, 2026
  • Slay the Spire
  • Slay the Spire 2

Happy Neowlloween, Slayers!

Much like last month, this Neowsletter is coming in a little later than usual. But this time it’s because we wanted to wait until we were able to offer some more solid info on when you can expect to see the next update.

Since August (aside from our 1 week studio break), we’ve been busy working on some big pieces of content that we’ve alluded to in past Neowsletters. All of these features were included in our Early Access roadmap at launch, but it wasn’t until the past couple of months that we’ve had the time to concentrate all of our focus on creating them. As you can imagine, these are big things that require a lot of design, UX work, art, animation, balancing, etc before they’re even ready for the experimental beta branch. They can’t be pumped out in a short amount of time nor can they be efficiently worked on in tandem with regular balance updates and bug fixes.

While we mentioned in the August Neowsletter that our next update would take a bit longer, we weren’t sure exactly when that would be… until now! The next big beta patch is currently planned to release on November 12th, which is just a couple of weeks away by the time you’re reading this. Of course, this new experimental content will be beta-exclusive at first as we work out the kinks, but the default/main branch will be brought up-to-date with all of the content, balance, and fixes that are live on the current and relatively stable beta branch version (v0.111.0). By this time next month, beta players will already have delved deep into the new stuff and will be sharing their opinions on why REDACTED should be buffed, REDACTED should be nerfed, and so on.

The new content will have more of an impact the less we say about it, so we’re going to keep those arrows in our quiver for now.

By the way… some of the team is heading to Shanghai for WePlay 2026!! There will be a Slay the Spire 2 booth with demos, exclusive merch, and interactive elements thanks to our friends at CiGA, as well as a Q&A and fan signing event with Casey! If you’re in town from November 21-22, we hope to see you there!

In the meantime, let’s answer some of the community’s questions that have been building up over the last while…

Q&A with C&A

Natalie asks: Loving Slay the Spire 2 so far, thank you so much for all the work you’ve put into it! Would it be possible for you to add modifiers to the custom run mode to force a specific Ancient to spawn at the start of Acts 2 + 3? Or a modifier that lets us pick which version of each act appears, like getting to choose between Overgrowth + Underdocks for Act 1? No worries if this isn’t possible due to coding reasons/it messing with the RNG, or if you just don’t want to add something like this. Casey: We’ll likely have to do a pass on Custom Runs near the v1.0 launch. While I want to add all the features and ways to explore the nooks and crannies of the game, controller support and minimum text size requirements make it difficult for us. This likely means that Desktop players + mods will get the most flexibility.

Oliwier asks: Hello megacrit team!! I’ve been wondering, is the yellow thing on the inklet a happy mouth or a single sad eye? Casey: It’s a single sad eye :(

HJain asks: I was wondering whether the team is currently thinking about long-term difficulty/replayability systems for co-op. Is something similar to Ascension planned or being considered for multiplayer? And are there plans for additional regions or alternative Acts that could make late-game runs more varied? Casey: We’ve found the reception to co-op really positive so we definitely want to add more longevity to it with many players letting us know that they exclusively play multiplayer too! As for additional Acts, that’s certainly in the pipes (within the Spire).

goodsoup asks: I have been loving STS2, especially multiplayer. I was wondering if we could get a system for trading gold. I understand why you may not want that for relics/potions, but i would love to be able to give someone else my remaining 23 gold esp. if we are about to fight a boss. Casey: If players can pool their resources to a single player it’s sometimes too easy to have one player snowball, which is a growth for characters throughout a run. The ability to get a relic that’s more suited for you in Treasure Rooms is already quite advantageous…

Jeremy asks: I like how your team has been willing to retune, rework, and modify content from the open access period to really match the vision of what you’re going for. With that being said, is there anything that you feel like “dang we really nailed that the first time around”? Anything from content/difficulty/art direction/etc. Thanks! Casey: Bash is the perfect card

Nicolas asks: how many feedbacks did you get since the beginning of EA? are you using a tool to cluster similar feedbacks or is this manageable by hand? Casey: The feedback all goes into a tool that we use though. We do a handful of both! The quantity of a specific type of feedback can tell us how frequently the issue occurs while we sometimes look for feedback on a specific card, encounter, etc :)

NeonWyvern asks: What is the best way we as players can word our in-game feedback (our F2s)? It must be hard to parse through all that player data, so how can we make our F2s easier for you to work with? Casey: It’s not that hard to work with, please keep using the in-game feedback tool. The in-game tool gives us the most data, lets us sort issues by category, helps us not open a ton of emails or hunt down Steam discussions. It’s the best way by far!

Kale asks: Would you ever be open to the idea of an official story adaptation of Slay the Spire (1 & 2)? Like the games being adapted into a graphic novel or even an animated series with a more cohesive story integrating all the lore and worldbuilding of the Spire and Preon? Casey: Yeah I’m open

Jeffreyjelly asks: Have you ever thought about collaborating with other games to create new characters at all? (This does sound like a balancing nightmare to add a bunch of cards though) Anthony: no

Aarfdark asks: I’ve been loving STS 2 so far and I can’t wait for what’s in store for us next! In the August Neowsletter, you said that y’all were working on experimental game modes. Could you tell us anything along the lines of how many you’re planning on making and whether they’re going to be focused around single player or multiplayer? Casey: The new game modes will work for both singleplayer and multiplayer but aside from that, you’ll have to wait… until November 12th!

Peeled Tomato asks: I’ve been loving Slay The Spire 1 and 2. My question is: Just like in StS 1 we were able to see the Beta Artstyle of cards, will we see them in StS 2 in Cards as well as in the Timeline? (I love the place holder Art in the Timeline that’s why!) Anthony: yes Casey: Did you just… say yes?
